The Hidden Stress Of Preparing For A Baby

One of the hardest parts of pregnancy and early parenting is the constant pressure to make the “right” decisions. Every search online opens hundreds of opinions, conflicting advice, product comparisons, parenting videos, and endless checklists.

Many parents spend hours researching bottles, bassinets, sleep products, feeding accessories, travel gear, nursery items, postpartum recovery products, and newborn essentials — only to feel more overwhelmed afterward.

Decision fatigue during pregnancy is real. New parents are already dealing with emotional stress, sleep changes, financial pressure, changing routines, and anxiety about the future.
